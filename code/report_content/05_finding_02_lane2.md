In Mali, if post-coup violence were jihadist resurgence, non-state killings would be rising. Instead, they are holding flat. Stanton's command-structure argument fits this asymmetry: actors with hierarchies escalate, while the loose groups the regime claims to fight do not [Stanton 2016]. Mali fits Kalyvas's indiscriminate-violence pattern in zones a regime cannot fully control [Kalyvas 2006]. Hultman documents this for rebels [Hultman 2007]. Marten describes Wagner's African expansion [Marten 2023].
