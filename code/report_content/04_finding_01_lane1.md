Figure 1 plots total monthly conflict events per country with first-coup (dashed) and Russian-deployment (dotted) markers. Mali sits at 100/month at the coup and 106 at Wagner, then climbs to 150–200/month from mid-2022. Our outcome variable (monthly civilian-targeted fatalities, derived from this series) tracks the same shape: Mali averages {mali_civ_pre_rate}/month pre-Wagner vs {mali_civ_post_rate} post, Burkina Faso {bf_civ_pre_rate} vs {bf_civ_post_rate}, Niger {niger_civ_pre_rate} vs {niger_civ_post_rate} (12-month window). Burkina Faso peaks at the 2022 coup and settles before Africa Corps arrives, so its rise is coup-driven, not Russian-driven. Figure 1b animates the same data year-by-year.
