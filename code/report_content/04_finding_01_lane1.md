Figure 1 plots total monthly conflict events per country with first-coup (dashed) and Russian-deployment (dotted) markers. Mali sits at 100/month at the August 2020 coup and 106 at the Wagner marker, then climbs to 150–200/month from mid-2022 onwards. Our outcome variable (monthly civilian-targeted fatalities, derived from this series) tracks the same shape: Mali rises from {mali_civ_pre_rate} to {mali_civ_post_rate}/month after {mali_wagner_arrival}, Burkina Faso from {bf_civ_pre_rate} to {bf_civ_post_rate} after {bf_wagner_arrival}, Niger from {niger_civ_pre_rate} to {niger_civ_post_rate} on a brief {niger_civ_n_post_months}-month window. Burkina Faso peaks at the 2022 coup and settles before Africa Corps arrives, so its rise is coup-driven, not Russian-driven. Figure 1b animates the same data year-by-year.
