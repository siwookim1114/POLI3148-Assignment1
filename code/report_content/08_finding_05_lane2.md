Sahelian counter-insurgency marks Fulani as a suspect category — operations target them regardless of JNIM membership, and targeting drives recruitment that retroactively confirms the category, a self-fulfilling loop Charbonneau documents [Charbonneau 2021; Charbonneau 2024]. As JNIM consolidated Mopti, contestation shifted south to Ségou (mixed Fulani–Bambara) — where Kalyvas predicts indiscriminate violence in zones a regime cannot fully control [Kalyvas 2006]. The variance pattern confirms regime-driven change, not cross-country drift.
