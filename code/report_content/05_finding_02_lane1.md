Figure 2 plots monthly civilian-targeted fatalities by perpetrator role across the AES core as stacked bars. In Mali's panel, the pre-Wagner bars are dominated by non-state killings. After Wagner, the state and external components grow sharply while non-state stays similar — state {mali_state_pre_rate}→{mali_state_post_rate}/month, external {mali_ext_pre_rate}→{mali_ext_post_rate}, non-state only {mali_nsag_pre_rate}→{mali_nsag_post_rate}. Burkina Faso's panel shows similar post-Africa-Corps state-force escalation in 2024–2025's red-dominant bars, though non-state also rises. Niger's brief post-window is too short for confident inference. Mali's asymmetry is the central empirical claim.
