These polyarchy collapses fit Roessler's coup–civil war trap, Mali the clearest case: a fractured officer corps since 2012, junta leaders purging rivals (Sanogo, Goïta), each purge widening the army's northern gap and inviting insurgent advance [Roessler 2011; Roessler 2016]. Reform would require trusting the officers just purged, so juntas chose force. Singh's faction-display logic explains why this produces civilian harm: visible sweeps against "internal enemies" lock in pro-coup officer loyalty as slow population-centric COIN cannot [Singh 2014]. That the Wagner-arrival breakpoint outperforms the French-exit confirms the substitution drove the change.
